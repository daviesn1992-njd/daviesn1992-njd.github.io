Support

ContractorCalc is a simple material and labor estimating tool for contractors and tradespeople.

Need help?

For launch support, email support@contractorcalc.app with your device, Android version, trade calculator, and a short description of the issue. Do not include payment-card information.

Common questions

  • Where are saved estimates? They are saved locally on the device in the current release.
  • Can I use the app offline? Yes. The calculator and recent local estimates are designed to work without an account or network connection.
  • Are material prices guaranteed? No. Starter prices are estimates. Confirm current supplier prices and site conditions before ordering.
  • How do I report a bug? Email the support address above and include the steps that reproduce it.
